# im-nothing

**Read this in:** [English](im-nothing.md) · [Русский](im-nothing.ru.md) · [中文](im-nothing.zh.md)

The project logo is a frame from the movie "Lucy" — after it, nobody smiles
anymore. The actress herself is gorgeous, with a gorgeous smile. I chose it
because it hits hard: if you've seen the film, you know this is the last smile.

The flashlight — just for fun. Not sure it even works, purely a demo.

And by the way: you still have to smile to verify the app works.
